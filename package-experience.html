PACKAGE 04 · LET BUYERS EXPERIENCE THE FUTURE

Property Experience™

Let buyers experience the property before they visit it.

BUILD MY PROPERTY EXPERIENCE SEE OUR PROJECTS

Transform architectural plans, renders and property data into an interactive sales experience that allows buyers to explore the development before completion.

Digital experiences can now combine interactive masterplans, unit exploration, 360° walkthroughs, digital twins and sales-gallery functionality in one connected system — this is the package that builds it.

Buyers don't want to read about the property. They want to see it.

A printed masterplan invites a buyer to find their unit; an interactive one lets them filter by orientation, floor and availability, then step straight into the unit they picked.

Ananta turns complex development information into an intuitive digital experience — one that works equally well on a sales-gallery touchscreen, a tablet in the field, or a link sent after a call.

THE PROCESS

HOW WE GET THERE

  1. MODEL — build the spatial and data model of the development
  2. DESIGN — design the masterplan, unit explorer and 360° flows
  3. DEVELOP — build the interactive experience
  4. INTEGRATE — connect it to the sales gallery and CRM
  5. DEPLOY — put it in front of buyers, on every device

IDEAL FOR

  • Large & masterplanned developments
  • Apartments & mixed-use developments
  • Villa estates
  • Resorts
  • Township projects
  • Pre-launch developments
  • Sales galleries

The masterplan, the unit explorer, the 360° tours and the sales gallery all read from the same digital twin — so complex developments become clearer opportunities.
